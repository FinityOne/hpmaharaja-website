Snapshot

I’m a founder and operator who builds things end to end — software, physical assets, and large live events. I’ve started and run four ventures across tech, real estate, experience design, and the non-profit world, which means I’ve had to own product, finance, hiring, vendors, and delivery under a real deadline with real money on the line.

What I bring to a team: zero-to-one product sense, comfort with ambiguity, and the operational discipline to actually ship and maintain what gets built.
